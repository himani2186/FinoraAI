About Finora AI

Redefining Personal Finance for Modern Earners

Traditional budgeting apps force you to manually type in every cup of coffee or parse confusing CSV spreadsheets. Finora AI was built to automate the tedious work and give you actionable, empathetic financial advice.

By combining Indian payment ecosystem intelligence (UPI, Netbanking alerts) with Google Gemini AI models, Finora transforms confusing debit alerts into clear, stress-free money management.

  • ✓ Zero Manual Entry Burden: Just copy your UPI bank SMS and let the regex engine do the categorization.
  • ✓ Contextual AI Conversations: Your financial advisor knows your monthly numbers and tailors advice to you.
  • ✓ Beautiful Glassmorphism Design: Clean, distraction-free purple aesthetic designed for fast daily check-ins.
