Our Mission

Making Financial Intelligence Effortless for Everyone

Finora AI was born from a simple observation: modern payment rails make spending money frictionless, but managing expenses remains manual, tedious, and stressful. We are here to change that.

🚀 Our Vision

To provide every earner and household with a personal, intelligent CFO powered by generative AI. We want to eliminate financial anxiety by turning raw bank alerts into clear, forward-looking financial decisions.

💡 Why Finora AI?

Unlike traditional spreadsheets or cumbersome budgeting software, Finora AI meets you where your money already flows: your UPI and SMS bank alerts. Combined with Google Gemini's reasoning capabilities, Finora provides actionable advice you can actually understand and follow.
